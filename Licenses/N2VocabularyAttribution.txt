Changes made for Haedok: removed duplicates and spelling/inflection aliases; standardized some uncommon spellings; corrected selected readings and glosses; wrote Korean meanings and original Japanese examples, example furigana and Korean translations; supplemented the selection with related reading-practice vocabulary; split the resulting data into six books of at most 300 entries.

This is an independently assembled N2 study and reading-practice selection. It is not an official or exhaustive JLPT vocabulary syllabus and is not endorsed by JLPT, Tanos or the reference repository's contributors. The JLPT does not publish a fixed official vocabulary list.
